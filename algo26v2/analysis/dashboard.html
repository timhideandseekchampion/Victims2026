05

What is definitively not there

Each of these families sits at or below the false-positive floor across the whole battery — the single-name behaviour of every instrument is a Gaussian random walk with drift and constant variance.

06

Frequency, dependence & learning

Spectral & wavelet
scipy.signal · PyWavelets
  • Spectral flatness 0.91 (1.0 = perfectly white); PSD slope +0.015.
  • 46/51 spectrally white — no dominant cycle at any horizon.
  • Wavelet energy falls off geometrically (most at 2-day scale) — the signature of white noise.
  • ALGO coherence is broadband (~0.33), not a tradeable frequency.
Copula dependence
Gaussian · t · Clayton · Gumbel · Frank
  • Best-fit families: Gaussian & Frank (both symmetric, zero tail dependence).
  • Lower-tail dep 0.168 vs upper 0.163 — asymmetry +0.005.
  • No Clayton / heavy-t wins → no crash co-movement to exploit.
Machine learning
14 sklearn + 10 deep models
  • Only tree ensembles beat shuffled baselines (HistGBM OOS R² +0.0045).
  • Clustering silhouettes ≈ 0 → structure is one continuous factor, not discrete sectors.
  • GraphicalLasso precision 54% zero → few dependencies beyond the factor.
  • CCA canonical corr 0.72 is in-sample only (every OOS model ≈ 0).

All mean-reversion — they diversify but share a driver. Exit band matters most: entering at 1σ and exiting at 0.5σ (not holding to 0) lifts the pair Sharpe from 0.6 → 4.35.
Combining pays off. Score = mean·SR²/(SR²+1): once Sharpe is high the fraction saturates, so stacking complementary reversion edges lifts PnL at little Sharpe cost. The edges are near-uncorrelated (ALGO-timing & lead-lag ≈ 0 with everything), so the first three stack raises Sharpe rather than diluting it.

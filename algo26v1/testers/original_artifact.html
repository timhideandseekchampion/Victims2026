Offline workbench generated from prices.txt. Overlays, regimes and portfolio stats are computed live in your browser. Built-in signals are illustrative — they show how entries/exits and portfolio risk behave, not a strategy to submit. Portfolio equity is an equal-weight (1/N), gross-of-fees mark-to-market of the selected signal across all instruments; Sharpe/Sortino/etc. are on those daily returns. The official, fee- and position-limit-aware numbers come from backtester.py --stats (and eval.py). To overlay your own book: python backtester.py --export-positions pos.csv then python dashboard.py --positions pos.csv and pick "Loaded positions".

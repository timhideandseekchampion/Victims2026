Regularisation sweep — the edge lives at light shrinkage

Walk-forward cross-sectional IC of the same linear model at increasing Ridge penalty. The signal sits in small cross-asset coefficients: OLS / light Ridge capture it; α≥10 shrinks them away and IC collapses below zero.
